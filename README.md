# ⚖️ Lexi — Legal Document Intelligence

> An AI-powered legal document analysis platform that helps users understand, analyze, compare, and query legal documents using evidence-based document intelligence.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://inter-major-project-legal-document.onrender.com/

---

## 📌 Overview

**Lexi** is an AI-powered Legal Document Intelligence application designed to make complex legal documents easier to understand and review.

Users can upload PDF or DOCX documents and analyze important information such as:

- Contract clauses
- Important dates
- Potential attention areas
- Obligations and terms
- Document differences
- Evidence-based answers to questions

Instead of manually searching through lengthy contracts, Lexi retrieves relevant document passages and provides answers grounded in the uploaded document.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** Lexi provides document-analysis assistance and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. Always consult a qualified legal professional for legal decisions.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 Document Upload

Upload legal documents in:

- PDF
- DOCX

Maximum supported file size:

- **25 MB**

---

### 🔍 Legal Document Analysis

Lexi analyzes uploaded documents and identifies:

- Common clause types
- Important dates
- Key contractual information
- Areas that may require attention

This helps users quickly understand the important parts of a document.

---
